Classify the finding against the component's existing job. First identify a concrete trigger, a promised result, and what actually happens. An unmet current promise, including an incorrect output consumed by an operator, is a real_defect. A proposal to retain, expose, or optimize additional information is an enhancement_opportunity when its only evidenced consequence is that the proposed capability is unavailable. An operator who would benefit from a feature is not evidence that the component promises it. Require a stated retention obligation, existing consumer contract, or observed operational failure before treating such a proposal as a defect. Cite the decisive observation. Use undetermined when conflicting evidence prevents the distinction.
