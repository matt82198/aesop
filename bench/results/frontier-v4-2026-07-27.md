## Caveats

1. **Refusal holes**: fable-5/opus-5 accuracy is computed on fewer tuples than sonnet/haiku/gpt; the common-task sensitivity column bounds the composition effect.
2. **Authoring bias**: ft101-ft130 were authored after v3 results were known (no per-model tuning; per-task table published for audit).
3. **Statistical scope**: equivalence at +/-10pp on THIS slice; smaller gaps are not ruled out; repo-authored, not an external benchmark.
4. **Single-shot shape**: clean-context single-shot QA with pinned answer formats; does not measure long-horizon agentic work (seam study is the next increment).
5. **Instrument revision**: v4 numbers are not comparable to v2/v3 absolutes (six repaired tasks + 30 new hard tasks).
